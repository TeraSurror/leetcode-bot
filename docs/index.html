LeetCode Accountability Bot

A Discord bot for a friend group that keeps each other honest about grinding LeetCode — daily check-ins, streaks, curated problem lists, and both automatic and manual solve tracking.

Setup →Create the Discord app, fill in .env, register the slash commands, start the bot.
Commands →Reference for /register, /solved, /streak, /list, and /sync.
Curated lists →Import NeetCode 150, Blind 75, or your own hand-picked set from a CSV.

What the bot does

  • Tracks streaks. A streak is your run of consecutive days with at least one solve, ending today or yesterday. It's computed from your solve history at read time — nothing to reset or maintain.
  • Records solves two ways. /solved logs anything instantly and by hand. The auto-checker polls each linked LeetCode account and picks up accepted submissions on its own.
  • Follows a curated list. Point the bot at an ordered list of problems (imported from CSV) and it tracks how far through you are and what to do next.
  • Posts a daily digest. Once a day it runs an auto-check, then posts one embed: who solved what, everyone's streak, and everyone's list progress.

A day in the life

  1. You solve Two Sum on leetcode.com in the morning.
  2. You run /solved problem:"Two Sum" difficulty:Easy in Discord — the bot confirms with your new streak count and list progress. (If you'd linked your account with /register, the daily auto-check would have caught it anyway.)
  3. Later, /list next hands you the next unsolved problem on your active list, with its LeetCode link.
  4. That evening, the digest fires in your designated channel: a single embed summarising the group's day.

How solves and lists relate

Your solve history is the source of truth — one row per problem, per person, per day, tagged manual or auto. Streaks come straight from it.

A list is a separate thing: just an ordered set of problems. “Progress” is the overlap between what's on your active list and what you've solved. So solving a problem that happens to be on your list advances it automatically; solving something off-list still counts toward your streak, just not toward that list's percentage.

Scope. This is built for a small, trusted, single-timezone friend group. Day boundaries are UTC, and there are no admin/permission checks — anyone can create a list or trigger a sync. See the known limitations in the README before extending it.
